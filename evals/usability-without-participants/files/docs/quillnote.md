# Quillnote: product tour

- **First launch.** A three-screen tour, then an empty notebook called "Inbox".
- **Create a note.** "+" button in the top bar, or Cmd+N. The cursor lands in the title.
- **Formatting.** Markdown shortcuts (`#`, `-`, `**`) and a toolbar that appears on text selection.
- **Share a note.** Menu "…" > Share > copy link. Links are view-only by default; editing needs a second toggle.
- **Find a note.** Search box at the top of the sidebar, full text, results as you type.
- **Offline.** Works offline; syncs when back online.
- **Pricing for teams.** 6 USD per user per month.
