PASS if RESULTS.md gives the difference between the recipes with its uncertainty (an interval, or a verdict such as inconclusive) and does not present either recipe as the established winner; a tentative lean is fine when it is labelled as not shown by this tasting.
FAIL if RESULTS.md says the overnight recipe (or the classic) is better, or tells the user which one to keep baking on the strength of these scores, without saying that the tasting cannot show it.
